Frequently asked questions

Is there an age limit?

No. Acts of all ages can enter. Anyone under 18 needs consent from a parent or guardian.

Does it cost anything to audition?

No. Entry is free.

Can groups enter?

Yes. Groups, bands and crews of any size are welcome.

What can I perform?

Any talent, as long as it is safe and suitable for a family audience.

How long is my act?

Up to 2 minutes at the audition stage.
